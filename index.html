    The local guide

    Our favorite spots near North Peak, for every age and every season.

    • Waterville Valley Resort

      All ages

      Family-friendly trails, lessons for every level and night skiing, with a full resort village at the base.

      About 20 min · Waterville ValleyMap

    • Loon Mountain

      All ages

      Big-mountain skiing across three peaks with a scenic gondola, plus summer adventures when the snow melts.

      About 25 min · LincolnMap

    • Tenney Mountain

      Families

      A laid-back local hill with snow tubing and terrain parks, a great pick for beginners and young kids.

      About 20 min · PlymouthMap

    • Cannon Mountain

      Experienced

      Steep, classic New England terrain with sweeping views over Franconia Notch.

      About 35 min · Franconia NotchMap

    • Squam Lakes Natural Science Center

      All ages

      A live animal trail with black bears, mountain lions and bald eagles, plus seasonal lake cruises.

      About 15 min · HoldernessMap

    • Clark's Bears

      Kids

      Trained bear shows, a steam train and old-time attractions, run by the Clark family since 1949.

      About 25 min · LincolnMap

    • Whale's Tale Waterpark

      All ages

      A giant wave pool, water slides and a lazy river for hot summer days.

      About 25 min · Lincoln · SummerMap

    • Polar Caves Park

      Kids

      Nine glacial caves to squeeze through, nature trails and animals to feed.

      About 20 min · RumneyMap

    • Flume Gorge

      All ages

      A two-mile walkway through a granite gorge at the base of Mount Liberty. Book tickets online ahead.

      About 30 min · Franconia NotchMap

    • Kancamagus Highway

      All ages

      New England's famous scenic drive, with waterfalls and overlooks. Unforgettable in foliage season.

      About 25 min · Starts in LincolnMap

    • Welch–Dickey Loop

      Teens & adults

      A favorite half-day hike with open granite ledges and big views of the valley.

      About 15 min · ThorntonMap

    • Lost River Gorge

      All ages

      Boardwalks, waterfalls and boulder caves to explore in Kinsman Notch.

      About 30 min · North WoodstockMap

    • Mad River Tavern

      Close by

      A hearty New England menu and live music, just down the road.

      CamptonMap

    • Covered Bridge Farm Table

      Date night

      Farm-to-table dining overlooking the Pemigewasset River and the Blair covered bridge.

      CamptonMap

    • Woodstock Inn Brewery

      Brewpub

      A big, festive brewpub in a late-1800s train station, brewing its own beer since 1982.

      About 25 min · North WoodstockMap

    • The Common Man

      Family-friendly

      Cozy, classic New Hampshire comfort food. A reliable dinner for the whole group.

      About 25 min · LincolnMap

    • Black Mountain Burger

      Après-ski

      Burgers and local beer after a day on the slopes at Loon.

      About 25 min · LincolnMap

    • Café Monte Alto

      Coffee

      A downtown café stop before a day of exploring.

      About 15 min · PlymouthMap

    Drive times are approximate from North Peak. Hours and seasons change, so check before you go.

    Why book direct instead of Airbnb?

    Booking on our website saves you 10% or more compared with third-party platforms, and you deal with us directly before and during your stay.

    Are pets allowed?

    Yes! Pets are welcome, so the whole family can come along.

    How many guests can stay?

    The Mountain View A-Frame sleeps up to 13 across 5 bedrooms (4 queen beds, 1 double and 4 singles). The Forest A-Frame has 5 bedrooms and 4 baths across 4,000 sq ft, with a sauna and kids playground. Our upcoming homes are 2- and 3-bedroom cabins.

    What is the cancellation policy?

    Cancel 30 days or more before arrival for a 100% refund of prepayments, or 7 days or more before arrival for a 50% refund. Cancellations within 7 days of arrival are non-refundable.

    When is payment due? Is there a security deposit?

    Payment is due in full at the time of reservation. No security deposit is required.

    What amenities are included?

    Parking, Wi-Fi with a dedicated workspace, a full kitchen, washer, bathtub, air conditioning and heating, a fire pit and a private hot tub. Smoke and CO detectors and a fire extinguisher are in every home.
